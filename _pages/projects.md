## News deserts and local information environments

How do gaps in local news coverage become gaps in people's access to civic information? At the Community Informatics Lab, I study **news deserts as information problems**, examining local coverage, information inequality, and the psychological distance between communities and the news available to them.

This work includes the collection and analysis of more than **40,000 news articles**, research on spatial patterns of city co-mentions, and my first-author iConference 2026 paper on psychological distance and the formation of news deserts.

[iConference 2026 paper](https://doi.org/10.47989/ir31iConf64264) · [ASIS&T local coverage study](https://doi.org/10.1002/pra2.1293) · [ASIS&T spatial analysis](https://doi.org/10.1002/pra2.1516)

## AI-mediated local news

Ongoing research on how conversational interfaces may shape people's relationships with local news. I examine psychological distance, information access, and civic engagement to understand how AI-mediated information environments may affect communities' access to locally relevant information.

## SAFETI: occupational safety and policy evaluation

A funded research collaboration with the Virginia Department of Labor and Industry. The project examines workplace safety, workers' compensation policies, and regulatory impacts using large-scale administrative datasets and predictive analytics.

## Behavioral Lifestyle Index at Parkland Health

I independently led a clinical data-science project at the Parkland Center for Clinical Innovation to develop a Behavioral Lifestyle Index for Parkland Health. Working with data covering approximately **500,000 patients and 9 million encounters**, I developed a reproducible approach to preprocessing, feature selection, and clustering, narrowing more than 100 candidate variables to approximately 40 indicators with clinical input.

## Personalized risk communication

Research on LLM-generated and personalized narrative messages, examining risk response effectiveness and willingness to take protective action during emergencies. This work led to my first-author IEEE SIEDS 2025 paper, which I presented, and a co-authored 2025 *IEEE Access* article.

[IEEE SIEDS paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/SIEDS65500.2025.11021204) · [IEEE Access article](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3600146)

## iOS geolocation for digital forensics

Research through the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative on the extraction and interpretation of iOS geolocation data, including stay-point detection in *Cache.sqlite* and the identification of meaningful movement patterns.

## Human–deer interactions and public health

Interdisciplinary research on human–wildlife encounters and potential zoonotic disease transmission, using systematically coded social-media data from Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram. The work contributed to a co-authored APHA conference presentation and multiple research posters.
