# Selected research projects

These are research themes and projects from my CV rather than a comprehensive list of software projects.

## News deserts and local information environments

Analysis of more than 40,000 news articles to understand local news coverage, information inequality, and the formation of news deserts. This work includes research on psychological distance, spatial patterns of city co-mentions, and ongoing work on AI-mediated access to local news. Relevant publications appeared in *Information Research* (iConference 2026) and *Proceedings of ASIS&T* (2025).

[Read iConference paper](https://doi.org/10.47989/ir31iConf64264) · [ASIS&T 2025 paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/pra2.1293)

## SAFETI: occupational safety and policy evaluation

A funded research collaboration with the Virginia Department of Labor and Industry. The project examines workplace safety, workers' compensation policies, and regulatory impacts with large-scale administrative datasets and predictive analytics.

## Behavioral Lifestyle Index at Parkland Health

A clinical data-science research project to develop a Behavioral Lifestyle Index, working with data from approximately 500,000 patients and 9 million encounters. The methodology included reproducible preprocessing, feature selection, and clustering to narrow over 100 variables to approximately 40 indicators.

## Personalized risk communication

Research on LLM-generated and personalized narrative messages to explore risk response effectiveness and willingness to take protective action during emergencies. This work led to a first-authored SIEDS 2025 paper and a co-authored 2025 *IEEE Access* article.

[Read SIEDS paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/SIEDS65500.2025.11021204) · [Read IEEE Access article](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3600146)

## iOS geolocation for digital forensics

Research through the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative on the extraction and interpretation of iOS geolocation data, including stay-point detection and identification of meaningful movement patterns.

## Human–deer interactions and public health

Interdisciplinary research into human–wildlife encounters and potential zoonotic disease transmission, using systematically coded social-media data across Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram. The research contributed to an APHA conference presentation and multiple posters.
