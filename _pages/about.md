# Tugce Burcu Gundogdu

**Computer Science Undergraduate Student and Researcher**  
George Mason University · Fairfax, Virginia  
*B.S. in Computer Science, expected May 2027*

I am an undergraduate researcher at George Mason University interested in how information and technology shape people's access to knowledge, experiences, and decisions. My research spans information inequality, AI-mediated information environments, computational social science, human–computer interaction, and public-interest technology.

At the Community Informatics Lab, I investigate local news and news deserts, including psychological distance, access to local information, and civic engagement. I also contribute to research using large administrative datasets to study workplace safety and policy impacts. My previous work includes personalized risk communication, digital forensics, healthcare data science, and computational approaches to public-health research.

## Selected research

**Local news, news deserts, and civic information**  
Research on the information environments of underserved communities, including the analysis of more than 40,000 news articles and investigations of psychological distance and AI-mediated news. [Explore research →](/research/)

**Workplace safety and policy evaluation**  
Computational analysis of workplace safety, workers' compensation policies, and regulatory impacts as part of the SAFETI research collaboration with the Virginia Department of Labor and Industry. [Explore research →](/research/)

**Health data science and risk communication**  
Research on a Behavioral Lifestyle Index using large-scale clinical data, and peer-reviewed work examining AI-generated and personalized risk communication messages. [Explore publications →](/publications/)
