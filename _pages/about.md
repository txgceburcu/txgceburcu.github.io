Information Science · Computational Social Science
{: .research-kicker}

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at George Mason University conducting research in **Information Science**, with a focus on information inequality, AI-mediated information environments, and computational approaches to socially consequential problems. I expect to graduate in May 2027.
{: .research-intro}

At the **Community Informatics Lab**, I study news deserts and local information environments: how psychological distance shapes people's relationships with local news, and what that means for civic information access. My work includes the analysis of more than **40,000 news articles** and ongoing research on AI-mediated local news and civic engagement.

I presented my first-author iConference 2026 paper [“Local news at a distance: Psychological distance and the formation of news deserts”](https://doi.org/10.47989/ir31iConf64264) at Edinburgh Napier University in the UK. I was also first author and presenter of our [IEEE SIEDS 2025 paper on personalized risk communication](https://doi.org/10.1109/SIEDS65500.2025.11021204).
