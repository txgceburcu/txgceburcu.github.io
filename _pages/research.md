# Research experience

My work combines computational methods with questions about information access, policy, health, and technology. The roles and descriptions below are based on my academic CV.

## Community Informatics Lab · George Mason University

**Undergraduate Research Assistant | December 2024 – Present**

- Investigate information inequality and news deserts through research on local news coverage; collected and analyzed more than **40,000 news articles** and contributed to peer-reviewed publications at ASIS&T and iConference.
- Conduct ongoing research on AI-mediated local news, examining how conversational interfaces may influence psychological distance, information access, and civic engagement.
- Contribute to **SAFETI**, a funded project conducted in collaboration with the Virginia Department of Labor and Industry, examining workplace safety, workers' compensation policies, and regulatory impacts with large-scale administrative data.

## Commonwealth Cyber Initiative (CCI) · George Mason University

**CCI Scholar – Research Fellow | 2025 – Present**

- Investigate techniques for extracting and interpreting iOS geolocation data for digital forensic investigations.
- Study stay-point detection and the identification of meaningful movement patterns.

## Parkland Center for Clinical Innovation (PCCI)

**Data Science Intern | May – August 2026**

- Independently led an advanced research project, originally scoped for two interns, developing a **Behavioral Lifestyle Index** for Parkland Health.
- Worked with data covering approximately **500,000 patients and 9 million clinical encounters**.
- Developed a reproducible data-processing, feature-selection, and clustering methodology, narrowing more than 100 candidate variables to approximately 40 indicators with clinical input.
- Presented the research to clinical and data-science audiences, including a session with more than 200 attendees.

## GMU Summer Team Impact Project (STIP)

**Undergraduate Research Assistant | May – November 2025**

- Selected for an interdisciplinary program exploring human–wildlife interactions and potential zoonotic disease transmission risks.
- Collected and systematically coded social-media data from Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram to study human–deer contact and associated public-health risks.
- Contributed to research presented at the 2025 APHA Annual Meeting and co-authored five research posters.

## Center for Social Complexity · George Mason University

**Undergraduate Research Assistant | December 2023 – May 2025**

- Studied LLM-generated and personalized risk communication messages and how they may influence protective behavior during emergencies.
- Analyzed survey responses across five messaging frameworks and three risk domains.
- Led three undergraduate researchers and collaborated with researchers at Old Dominion University, contributing to peer-reviewed publications.

**Computational approaches:** NLP and LLM-assisted text analysis; geographic and social-network analysis; clustering; feature selection; predictive modeling; statistics; data visualization.  
**Programming and data:** Python (pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), R, SQL, Spark, Java, C.  
**Research tools:** Qualtrics, Stata, Tableau, Git/GitHub, Jupyter, Linux/Unix.
